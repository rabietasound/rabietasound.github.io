The name
ra·bie·ta

/ rrah-byeh-tah / noun · Spanish

  1. A temper tantrum.
  2. Ricky's alter ego behind the decks: a Miami DJ who plays loud, fun, mezcal-forward sets that get the whole room moving.

Music has been Ricky's driving force since the day a song sparked something in him. After years around the industry, 2026 is the year he stepped up as Rabieta, with a weekly Friday run at La Yolanda Mezcalería and a steady rotation of Miami's best bars and taco spots.

"The name Rabieta is my alter ego, meaning temper tantrum in Spanish. That's me!"— Ricky Rabieta
